About Azat Tours

Azat means “free”, run by locals since 2019.

Azat is our founder's name and the Kyrgyz word for free. He has guided these valleys since 2019 with a small local team, and built Azat Tours so you feel free here, and at home. Here's what that means for your trip:

Real local guides

Every trip is led by Kyrgyz guides who know the passes, the families and the safest river crossings, not a script.

Tailor-made by default

Tell us your dates, pace and budget. We design an itinerary that fits you, no rigid package tourism.

Safety first

Vetted vehicles, first-aid-trained guides, satellite comms in remote areas and 24/7 support back in Bishkek.

Community tourism

We partner with herder families and village hosts, so your money stays in the mountains it came from.

Easy from abroad

Visa-free for 60+ nationalities. We help with logistics, airport pickup, SIM cards and everything in between.

Fair, honest pricing

Transparent quotes, no hidden extras, and a best-price promise. Enquire free, pay only when you're ready.

Yes. Kyrgyzstan is one of Central Asia's most welcoming and stable destinations, with very low crime against travellers. Our guides are first-aid trained, vehicles are vetted, and we carry satellite communication in remote areas.

June to September is ideal for trekking, horse treks and yurt stays, when high passes are open and the jailoo (summer pastures) are green. May and October are beautiful and quieter for lower-altitude and cultural trips.

We grade every tour from easy to hard. There are gentle day tours and lakeside trips with almost no walking, right up to high-altitude expeditions. Tell us your level and we'll match the route, or build one for you.
