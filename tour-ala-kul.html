Why you'll love this trek

This is the trek people fly to Kyrgyzstan for. In a week you climb from pine forest into raw high mountains, crest a 3,900 m pass, and drop to a lake so vividly turquoise it doesn't look real — then reward your legs in natural hot springs under the stars.

Day-by-day itinerary

Tap any day to expand. Every itinerary can be adjusted to your pace and fitness.

We meet you at Tamchy/Bishkek and transfer to Karakol, the trekking hub on the east shore of Issyk-Kul. Evening gear check, route briefing and a welcome dinner of local Dungan cuisine. Overnight in a comfortable guesthouse.

Drive to the trailhead and walk up the Karakol river through spruce forest and meadows. First night under canvas at Sirota meadow camp, surrounded by peaks. Crew cooks a hot dinner.

The highlight. A steady climb brings the turquoise lake into view, then up to the Ala-Kul Pass (3,900 m) for a jaw-dropping panorama before descending toward the springs. Tough but unforgettable.

A gentler descent into the Arashan valley. Afternoon free to soak tired muscles in natural hot pools with a view of Palatka Peak. Night in a rustic mountain guesthouse.

Walk out of the valley and transfer to the southern shore of Issyk-Kul. Optional swim in the world's second-largest alpine lake and a relaxed lakeside evening.

Visit the dramatic red "Seven Bulls" cliffs and the surreal "Fairy-Tale" canyon. Time with a local family for fresh kymyz and felt-making before your last mountain sunset.

Scenic drive back along the Issyk-Kul shore to the airport, with stops for photos and souvenirs. Hugs, contacts swapped, and a head full of mountains.
