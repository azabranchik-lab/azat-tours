## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Founder-led Kyrgyz tour company (Azat = "free" in Kyrgyz) running small-group and tailor-made adventures, so travellers feel free and at home in Kyrgyzstan.
**Founder:** Azat — has personally guided travellers across Kyrgyzstan since 2019; works with a team of local guides. Brand promise springs from his name: *azat* = free.
**What it does:** Azat Tours plans and runs trekking, horse treks, yurt stays and culture trips across Kyrgyzstan for international travellers. Trips are either small-group set departures or fully tailor-made. A local team handles itinerary, guides, transport, stays and on-trip support; travellers enquire free and pay only once the plan is confirmed.
**Product category:** Inbound adventure / cultural tour operator (Kyrgyzstan & Central Asia). The "shelf": *Kyrgyzstan tours*, *Kyrgyzstan trekking*, *Central Asia adventure travel*.
**Product type:** Service (DMC / tour operator) with a lead-gen website + Telegram admin.
**Business model:** Custom-quoted trips. Free enquiry, no prepayment to enquire; small deposit on confirmation, balance before/on arrival. Revenue = margin on trips.

## Target Audience
**Target customers:** Independent international travellers (not domestic), 25–60, from EU/UK/USA/Canada/Australia/East Asia. Couples, small friend groups, solo adventurers, active over-50s. Mid-to-upper budget, experience-driven.
**Decision-makers:** The traveller themselves (B2C). One person usually researches and books for the group.
**Primary use case:** "I want to experience Kyrgyzstan's mountains and nomad culture authentically and safely, without organising the logistics myself in a country I've never been to."
**Jobs to be done:**
- Reach remote, hard-to-DIY places (Song-Kul, Ala-Kul, high jailoo) with a guide who knows them.
- Travel authentically — sleep in yurts, meet herder families — without it feeling staged.
- Outsource the risk and hassle (permits, transport, safety, language) to trusted locals.
**Use cases:** Summer trekking holiday; horse trek like a nomad; Issyk-Kul + culture loop; one tailor-made multi-week Central Asia leg; short day tours from Bishkek for business/stopover travellers.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Travellers want the *real* Kyrgyzstan — high mountains, yurts, nomad culture — but it's remote, low-infrastructure, language-barriered, and hard to plan or trust from abroad.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Big international agencies: generic, expensive, not actually on the ground, impersonal.
- Pure DIY: logistics, safety, permits and language are daunting; you miss the hidden places.
- Faceless OTA listings: no real human, no local knowledge, hard to trust with prepayment.
**What it costs them:** Wasted holiday on a mediocre/over-touristed trip, safety risk, overpaying, or never going at all.
**Emotional tension:** "Will this be safe? Will it be authentic or a tourist trap? Can I trust a company I found online in a country I've never visited?"

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Other Kyrgyz/Central Asia operators (e.g. Kyrgyz-based DMCs, CBT networks, regional adventure brands) — many have dated sites, weak English, slow replies, or feel transactional.
**Secondary:** Global adventure brands (Intrepid, G Adventures, Exodus) — same JTBD, but fixed group packages, higher price, not local, less flexible.
**Indirect:** DIY independent travel (guidebooks, hostels, marshrutkas) — cheaper and "freer," but high effort, language barrier, and you miss remote highlights.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- 100% local Kyrgyz team — guides who grew up here, not subcontracted strangers.
- Tailor-made by default — itinerary built around your dates, pace, budget.
- Low-friction trust — free to enquire, no prepayment, a real human replies in 24h.
- Community model — money stays with herder families and village hosts.
**How we do it differently:** We design AND run trips ourselves, on the ground, as locals — not a reseller.
**Why that's better:** Access to real places, genuine culture, faster honest answers, money that supports the mountains.
**Why customers choose us:** Authenticity + safety + a human relationship, at a fair, transparent price.

**Anti-persona:** Pure budget backpacker who wants the cheapest possible DIY trip; luxury 5-star-resort traveller who doesn't want yurts/horses/rustic stays; package-bus mass tourist.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Frustration with generic packages, untrustworthy listings, the stress of DIY-ing a remote country.
**Pull:** Authentic local team, tailor-made trips, free low-risk enquiry, "they grew up there."
**Habit:** Defaulting to known global brands or familiar DIY booking sites.
**Anxiety:** "What if a smaller local company isn't reliable / what if I prepay and something goes wrong?" → answered by no-prepayment enquiry + reviews + named team.
