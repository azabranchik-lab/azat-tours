Good to know

Questions, answered

Everything first-time visitors ask before booking. Still unsure? Message us — we reply fast.

Citizens of 60+ countries — including the EU, UK, USA, Canada, Australia, Japan and more — can enter visa-free for up to 60 days. We'll confirm your specific case and help with any paperwork when you enquire.

Yes. Kyrgyzstan is one of Central Asia's most welcoming and stable destinations, with very low crime against travellers. Our guides are first-aid trained, vehicles are vetted, and we carry satellite communication in remote areas.

June to September is ideal for trekking, horse treks and yurt stays, when high passes are open and the jailoo (summer pastures) are green. May and October are beautiful and quieter for lower-altitude and cultural trips.

We grade every tour from easy to hard. There are gentle day tours and lakeside trips with almost no walking, right up to high-altitude expeditions. Tell us your level and we'll match the route — or build one for you.

Enquiring is completely free with no prepayment. Once your itinerary is confirmed, we take a small deposit to reserve guides and yurts, with the balance due before or on arrival. Bank transfer and card both accepted.
