These mountains raised us.

And nothing makes us happier than sharing them. We're a small family of Kyrgyz guides who grew up between Issyk-Kul and the Tian Shan. This is home — come and see it the way we do.

Our story

It started with a grandmother's yurt

Most of us knew the mountains before we knew how to read. Summers on the jailoo in a grandmother's yurt, the smell of woodsmoke and horses, kymyz poured for everyone who passed by — in our culture a stranger at the door isn't an interruption, it's a gift.

We started BeLocal for a simple reason. We kept meeting travellers who had seen Kyrgyzstan from a bus window but never really met it. So we began taking a few of them the old way — on horseback to Song-Kul, into valleys our families have grazed for generations, to tables where the beshbarmak never ends and the questions run late into the night.

We're proudly local. Our guides are from Karakol, Naryn and Bishkek. We sleep in the same yurts you do, we know which pass is safe when the weather turns, and we treat every traveller the way our grandparents taught us to treat a guest. A big agency booking you from an office abroad simply can't give you that.

"We don't sell tours. We open the door to our home and say — come in."

Leading a horse trek to Song-Kul, summer on the jailoo

Why we do this

Our mission

To let you feel Kyrgyzstan from the inside — its high passes, felt yurts and open tables — and to make sure the mountains and families we love are better off for every traveller we bring. We want our children to inherit these valleys greener, and every herder who hosts you to be glad we came.
